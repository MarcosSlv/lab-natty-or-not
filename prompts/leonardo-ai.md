# Prompts para o Leonardo.AI

Gere uma imagem por vez. Use o **estilo comum** seguido de **uma única cena**. Cada combinação fica abaixo de 2.000 caracteres. Para imagens internas, use formato horizontal 3:2; para capa, vertical 2:3. A escolha do formato depende da interface disponível na ferramenta.

## Estilo comum

Modern flat editorial illustration for a Brazilian personal finance ebook. Subtle paper texture, clean geometric shapes, forest green, sage green, warm cream, and muted navy blue palette. Warm, calm, approachable atmosphere, everyday setting, realistic and achievable financial progress. Balanced composition, clear focal point, generous negative space, consistent professional book illustration style. No text, letters, numbers, currency symbols, logos, watermarks, signatures, distorted hands, extra fingers, clutter, neon colors, luxury imagery, gambling, cryptocurrency symbols, photorealism, or 3D rendering.

## 1. Capa

A relatable adult at a tidy home desk, calmly organizing a notebook, calculator, receipts, and small stacks of coins. A small growing plant represents gradual progress. Vertical 2:3 composition, with generous empty space in the upper third for the title to be added later. Keep all important elements away from the edges. Do not render any title or other text.

## 2. Abertura

A thoughtful adult by a window, preparing to organize personal finances with a notebook, pencil, and a small pile of receipts. Soft morning light. Convey a calm first step and hope grounded in everyday life. Horizontal 3:2 composition.

## 3. Diagnóstico e gastos

Top-down view of a tidy desk. Two hands sort receipts into small groups beside an open notebook with abstract colored blocks, a calculator with a blank display, and a few coins. Emphasize clarity and observation. Horizontal 3:2 composition.

## 4. Orçamento

## 5. Dívidas

## 6. Reserva

A transparent savings jar containing a modest amount of coins is protected by a green umbrella. Gentle raindrops fall outside the umbrella, with a small house subtly in the background. Communicate preparation and gradual security. Horizontal 3:2 composition.

## 7. Metas

## 8. Rotina
